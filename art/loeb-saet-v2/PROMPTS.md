# Korrigeret løbecyklus — prompts

28. oktober 2026. Indbygget imagegen med ægte gennemsigtighed. De to
valgte RGBA-masters er kopieret uændret til denne mappe.

Reference 1: den tekniske poseguide i poseguide.png. Reference 2:
../stilproeve/stilproeve-01.png. De gamle løbe-poser blev ikke brugt som
reference i de valgte kald, fordi de gentog samme benfase.

Tidligere forsøg og en senere armvariant blev fravalgt efter billedkontrol;
de indgår ikke i spillet. Poseguiden er et teknisk diagram, ikke spilegrafik.

## Dreng

Kilde: exec-afba333c-0c22-434e-841f-400e55ec84fd.png

```text
Use case: sketch-to-render.
Image1 is a TECHNICAL POSE GUIDE and must dictate every limb position in the output. Image2 is a STYLE and CHARACTER reference. Convert the skeletons in image1 into nine clean polished full-body illustrations of the boy from image2, arranged in the exact same 3x3 cells. Genuine transparent background.
Do not invent generic running poses. TRACE THE POSE GUIDE. Navy lines are the near limbs and gray lines are far limbs, not clothing colors. Keep the near limb connected to the same near shoulder/hip and drawn over the far limb throughout. Where the guide's navy LEG points backward LEFT, draw the near leg backward LEFT. Especially middle-center and middle-right: the near leg must be BACKWARD, NOT forward. The near hand is FORWARD in these cells. Bottom-left: near heel folds up behind, while far leg is planted. Middle-left and bottom-middle are upright passing phases, not squats. Full alternating run cycle. Every face and shoe toe faces RIGHT.
Boy identity: tousled brown hair, warm cheerful face, red short-sleeved T-shirt, blue shorts, white socks, red and white sneakers. Match boy at left of image2, same body/head scale in every cell. Make his torso, hairstyle and shoes consistent with image2. Dark warm outlines, bright sunny highlights, painterly cel shading, rounded child-friendly game character. Head size constant, small natural bob, no giant leaps.
All nine drawings fully separate with generous clear margins. Upper-left is neutral calibration. Remove all guide text, numbers and marks. No labels, no outlines of panels, no white background, no checkerboard, no floor, no shadows, no extra limbs. Output only finished transparent RGBA artwork. The pose guide's geometry is the priority, not generic action illustration conventions.
```
