# Superheltelanding — prompts

28. oktober 2026. Indbygget imagegen i to separate kald med reel
RGBA-gennemsigtighed. De valgte PNG-filer er kopieret uændret.

Referencer: den tekniske poseguide i poseguide.png, de eksisterende
superhelteark i ../superhelt-saet/ og den godkendte stilprøve i
../stilproeve/stilproeve-01.png. Poseguiden er et diagram til produktion,
ikke grafik der indlæses i spillet.

## dreng

Kilde: exec-51f07b91-22c7-403a-8955-97d82ee9856c.png

```text
Use case: stylized-concept.
Asset type: transparent production sprite sheet for a children's side-scrolling game, superhero landing animation.
Input image1: EXACT POSE GUIDE. Image2: SAME CHARACTER IDENTITY, scale and purple/gold superhero costume. Image3: approved illustration STYLE.
Create exactly FOUR separate full-body drawings in a uniform 2x2 square sheet. Actual RGBA transparency. The pose guide's gray/navy colors and lettering are technical only: do not copy them. Use the same finished child from image2.
Top-left frame0: standing relaxed neutral calibration, like frame0 of image2, same head/body proportions. Purple cape with gold edging, gold star on shirt.
Top-right frame1: entering landing from horizontal flight. The upper body leans backward diagonally, knees drawn FORWARD toward screen right, BOTH shoes clearly in FRONT OF the hips (right of hips), rather than trailing behind as in flight. Like gently braking in the air. Arms bend toward front for balance, cape streams to LEFT. Trace guide's overall posture.
Bottom-left frame2: torso rises to nearly vertical, thighs still swing forward, knees bent in front and lower legs extend FORWARD AND DOWN. Both shoes ahead of the hips, preparing a feet-first landing. Arms spread naturally for balance; cape still trails left. Trace guide.
Bottom-right frame3: upright just before contact, feet pointing right and now DOWN below the hips, knees softly bent to receive the landing, arms balancing. Cape settles down behind the back. NOT a deep squat and NOT a running stride. Trace guide.
Keep every head, face and shoe toe facing SCREEN RIGHT. Lock head diameter, torso length, arm/leg thickness, hands and shoes to neutral calibration in ALL FOUR drawings. The more compact airborne posture must be shorter because of bent knees, not because it is scaled up/down to fit a box. Identical anatomical scale in all cells. All drawings fully contained in their cell with generous margins including long cape, hair, hands and shoes. No cross-cell overlap.
Match image2's original child and costume exactly, image3's warm lighting, strong dark colored outline, bright colors and soft painterly cel shading. No scene, ground, shadows, labels, text, numbers, panel lines, logos, motion streaks, checkerboard, extra limbs or accessories. Output only these four finished character sprites on genuine transparent background.
Character: the cheerful boy with tousled brown hair, red T-shirt, blue shorts, white socks and red/white sneakers, purple cape with gold edging and a gold star on red shirt.
```
